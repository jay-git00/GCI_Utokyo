## 1. Why Study Data Science?
“Why should I study data science?”
“Because data is everywhere, no matter what field you study. Learning data science helps you find patterns, test ideas, and make better decisions using evidence.”

### Data is Growing Exponentially
1 ZB (Zettabyte) = 1,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 bytes
Projected to reach 527 ZB by 2029.

### Data Already Shapes Decision Across Industries
* **Finance:** Trading, Risk management, Fraud detection
* **Retail:** Forecast demand, Recommend items
* **Marketing:** Compare advertisements, Verification of measures
* **Games:** User behavior analysis, Improve retention
* **Healthcare / Disaster:** Plan transport, Improve accessibility, Public services
* **Manufacturing:** Detect defects, Schedule repairs
* **Security:** Anomaly detection, Investigate access
* **Others:** Internet, Broadcasting, Human Resources, Education, Sports, Law, etc.

### More Data Does Not Guarantee Better Knowledge
CRISP-DM Cycle:
1. Business understanding
2. Data understanding
3. Data preparation
4. Modeling
5. Evaluation
6. Expansion

*Example: Food trucks*
* Data: Yesterday, the food truck sold 40 sandwiches. We sold all the sandwiches.
* Questions: When did we sell out? Is this pattern repetitive? How are the buying patterns of the customers?
* Actions: Order more? Discount late? Change opening hours?

**Data matters when it leads to actions.**

### Ask What is Missing
Relying solely on the data you have can lead to incorrect decisions.
* **Visible data:** database, file, etc.
* **Hidden data:** abstract concepts including those that are not available to make data out of.

### Someone Must Connect the Problem to the Data
Define the problem first. Then, translate the problem into something that can be checked.
* **Example:** "We want to improve call center sales efficiency..." -> "Let’s measure repeat contacts within 30 days. We need case IDs and follow-ups."

### Skill Set of a Data Scientist
* **Domain / Business Knowledge:** Ability to organize and resolve business issues by understanding their underlying context, drivers, and implications.
* **Data Science Skills:** Ability to understand and use information science knowledge such as information processing, AI, and statistics.
* **Data Engineering Skills:** Ability to use, implement, and operate data science in a meaningful way.

## 2. Data Science as Science

### Data Science Runs the Same Loop as Science
* **Hypothesize:** State an explanation and predict an outcome.
* **Experiment:** Collect evidence through a fair comparison.
* **Analyze:** Compare the result with the prediction.
* **Revise:** Revise the hypothesis using the findings.

### Case Study | 7-ELEVEN (Item-by-item management)
1. **Observe:** Combine recent sales, weather and local events.
2. **Hypothesize:** Predict demand for one item, at one store and time.
3. **Order & sell:** Choose the order quantity and arrange the display.
4. **Check & revise:** Compare forecast and sales; revise the next order. Repeat.

### AI Coding Accelerates Iteration
Translating your problem, building hypotheses, and actually solving the problems is still your task.
*“Asking the right question is the hardest part of science.”* - Demis Hassabis

### 3 Keys in Data Science
1. **Hypothesis-Driven Objectives:** Problem solving in business
2. **Actionable Insights:** New projects, ideas
3. **Methods Comparison:** Statistics, data science

Solve real-world problems around you and extract value by scientifically analyzing data-driven insights.
