## Calendar
Check the schedule for upcoming Lectures & Office Hours

**What is Office Hour?**
Office Hours are optional, interactive sessions held several times a week on a varying schedule.
Sessions may be hosted by TAs, lecturers, or past course graduates.
Topics vary and may include course Q&A, specialist talks, and Competition tips.
Check the calendar below for the date, time, and topic of each session.
Once the date and time are confirmed, the event will be added to the lecture calendar.
Please note that the schedule is subject to change. For the latest information, please refer to the announcements on Slack.

**Copy the calendar to your own Google Calendar and view it in your time zone**
Click “Add to Google Calendar” at the bottom left of the calendar.
Follow the prompt to add the “GCI World” calendar.
Once added, it will appear in your Google Calendar and events will be shown in your local time zone.
